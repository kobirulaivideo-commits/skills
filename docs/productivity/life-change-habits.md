## What it does

`life-change-habits` turns one change you want into a seven-step **habit experiment**: a small behaviour, a reliable cue, a low-friction score, and a scheduled review.

It does not promise a permanent transformation or prescribe a universal number of days. It designs a temporary experiment around your real baseline, then treats the results — including lapses — as data for the next adjustment.

## When to reach for it

You invoke this by typing `/life-change-habits` — the agent won't reach for it on its own.

Reach for it when a goal such as “exercise more,” “use my phone less,” or “write regularly” is meaningful but too vague to act on consistently. Bring one life change; the skill will help you choose one behaviour instead of launching an exhausting bundle.

## A habit experiment, not a character test

The skill separates an ordinary **target rep** from a tiny **minimum rep**, anchors the behaviour to a stable cue, and changes the environment before asking for more motivation. If the goal is to stop doing something, it designs a replacement rep so success is observable rather than defined only by an absence.

Scoring stays deliberately plain: did the behaviour happen when the opportunity appeared? A missed rep is recorded, not punished. At the scheduled review you keep, shrink, reshape, or replace one part of the plan based on what actually created friction.

## It's working if

- You can say exactly what counts as one rep.
- The plan names when and where the rep starts.
- A difficult day still has a realistic minimum rep.
- One quick mark records each real opportunity.
- The review has a date and can change the plan without resetting your identity or resolve.
