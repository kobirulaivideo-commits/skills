Add the user-invoked **`life-change-habits`** productivity skill. It turns one desired life change into a seven-step habit experiment with an observable target and minimum rep, a stable cue, an environment change, an obstacle and lapse plan, behaviour-based scoring, and a scheduled keep/shrink/reshape/replace review. The skill is included in the plugin, docs, indexes, and `ask-matt` router.
